# Copywriting guide — source of truth for site copy

Read before writing any new marketing/site copy, page content, or section headlines.
Combines [company-profile.md](./company-profile.md) (the brand's own PDF voice) with
[onboarding-brief.md](./onboarding-brief.md) (the client's direct, more recent notes on
what they want the *new* site to sound and feel like).

## Voice

- Confident, plain, "one stop shop" — not corporate-stiff, not casual either. Company
  profile tone markers: "fuss-free", "friendly and reliable", "care and love."
- Client's own framing for the new site skews more premium/minimal than the PDF brochure
  copy: "clean, stylish, cool to reflect our personality," vibe = **Luxury**. Lean the new
  site's *original* copy (headlines, straplines, section intros) toward this tighter,
  more confident register — reserve the fuller "fuss-free... care and love" brochure voice
  for longer-form content like About Us or Services, where the PDF text can be reused more
  directly.
- "About Us" is explicitly meant to be **brief** ("a brief outline") — don't pad it out
  with the full company-profile.md narrative; summarize.

## Confirmed exact copy

- **Home page strapline (locked):** "Integrity, reliability, and performance." — appears
  both as the Page 1 cover quote in company-profile.md and named directly in the
  onboarding form. Use verbatim, don't paraphrase.
- **Services list (verbatim, reuse as-is, typos included per source):** Pre construction
  Planning, Design & Build, Space Planning, Interior Design, 3D Visualisation, MEP Design,
  Fit-out, Refurbishment, FF&E procurement, Furniture consultancy and supply, Upholstery,
  Commercial branding, Signage, Vinal wrap, Faux Plants and Aftercare. See
  company-profile.md Page 3 for source and the "Known inconsistencies" section before
  quoting figures/claims publicly.
- **Key facts to draw on:** 30+ years experience, in-house team covers every stage
  (pre-construction → handover), D&B projects ~30% quicker than traditional procurement,
  based in Hoxton, London.

## Things to avoid

- No em dashes anywhere in site copy (headlines, straplines, body text, buttons). Use a
  comma, period, semicolon, or rewrite the sentence instead.
- Don't invent new taglines/straplines for Home — one is already confirmed above.
- Don't silently "correct" typos or inconsistencies carried over from company-profile.md
  or onboarding-brief.md without flagging it to the user first (both docs preserve `[sic]`
  markers for exactly this reason).
- Don't write long-form copy for Booking, Contact, or Testimonials — no brief exists yet
  for these; keep placeholder copy minimal until the client gives direction.
