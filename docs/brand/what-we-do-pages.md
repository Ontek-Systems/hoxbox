# "What We Do" Pages — Information Architecture

Decided IA for the site's services structure, reconciled from the full 15-item service
list in [company-profile.md](company-profile.md) against how competitors frame their
offering (client-facing outcomes, not internal techniques). Use this list verbatim
whenever asked to "build the What We Do pages" — don't re-derive the split from scratch.

## Why this split

Hoxbox's own service list mixes two altitudes: what a client is buying (office design,
fit-out, refurbishment) and how it gets delivered (MEP design, space planning, vinyl
wrap). Client-facing pages should be pitched at the first altitude — nobody searches
"MEP design," they search "office fit out." Granular techniques become supporting detail
*within* the relevant page, not pages of their own.

Dropped from the original 15-item list: Workplace Consultancy (not offered), Residential
(not offered), MEP Design / Space Planning / Upholstery / Commercial Branding / Vinyl
Wrap (folded in as detail bullets on the relevant page below, not standalone pages),
FF&E Procurement (folded into Furniture — FF&E means Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment;
it's procurement logistics, not a distinct service).

## The 8 "What We Do" pages

These are the ones that appear in the homepage scroll/carousel sequence, in this order:

1. **Office Design** — the planning/layout phase: space planning, interior design
   direction, concept development before build starts.
2. **Office Fit-Out** — turning the design into a built space: construction, MEP
   coordination, installation, handover.
3. **Office Refurbishment** — upgrading or reconfiguring an existing occupied or
   part-occupied space, rather than a from-scratch fit-out.
4. **Small Works** — lighter-touch, faster-turnaround jobs that don't need a full D&B
   programme.
5. **Pre-Construction Planning** — feasibility, budgeting, scheduling, risk assessment
   before any work starts (see company-profile.md page 4 for the full detail set).
6. **Interior Design** — the design-led, aesthetic/spatial side, distinct enough from
   Office Design's planning focus to stand alone.
7. **3D Visualisation** — rendering/visualising a scheme before commitment, a genuine
   client-facing deliverable in its own right.
8. **Aftercare** — post-handover support; promoted to a full page per client decision
   even though it's a small offering, because it reinforces the "single accountable
   partner" positioning from the company profile.

## The 4 sub-brand pages

Linked off the "What We Do" pages ("more detail") rather than included in the main
scroll sequence. These map to Hoxbox's existing sub-brand lockups (company-profile.md
pages 16–19):

- **Furniture** — consultancy, supply, and FF&E procurement (merged in; FF&E has no
  separate page).
- **Joinery** — bespoke in-house joinery (kitchens, wardrobes, doors, cabinetry).
- **Plants** — faux plant design and supply.
- **Signage** — interior signage, lightboxes, acrylic lettering, vinyl graphics,
  wayfinding, wall murals.

## Explicitly not standalone pages

- MEP Design → detail bullet on Office Fit-Out
- Space Planning → detail bullet on Office Design
- Upholstery → detail bullet on Furniture
- Vinyl Wrap → detail bullet on Signage
- Commercial Branding → detail bullet on Signage (or Office Design, tbc at copy stage)
- FF&E Procurement → folded into Furniture page, not named separately
- Workplace Consultancy, Residential → not offered by the client, excluded entirely
