## Reconciling with company-profile.md

The company-profile.md (PDF) transcription and this onboarding form share the same
"WELCOME TO HOXBOX..." business description and the same services list verbatim — they're
the same underlying brand copy, not two different voices. Where they diverge is the *art
direction for the new site*, which only exists in this form:

- Black/white base palette with division colors used **minimally** (this form) — this
  narrows, not contradicts, the multi-color division-logo system already documented in
  [logo-system.md](./logo-system.md). Division colors identify sublines/logos; they are
  not meant to flood the page.
- "Bold" font style + "Luxury" vibe + "clean, stylish, cool" — a firmer, more minimal
  brief than the current site, which the client explicitly flags as having "oversized
  fonts to go."
- Confirmed strapline for the Home page: **"Integrity, reliability, and performance."**
  (this also appears standalone on Page 1 of company-profile.md as a cover-page quote, so
  it's now confirmed as the intended hero strapline, not just a cover flourish).
